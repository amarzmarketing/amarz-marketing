Our process

We don't start posting. We start understanding.

After the deal is closed, we don't immediately take over your handles and start publishing. We first build the business context that makes the marketing meaningful.

01

Business Discovery

We understand your products/services, customers, goals, strengths, challenges, positioning and how the business operates.

02

Competitor Research

We study competitors, offers, positioning, content and customer communication to find gaps and opportunities.

03

Monthly Plan

We build a month-long marketing plan covering content themes, campaigns, offers, platforms and priorities.

04

Team Strategy Meeting

Our team discusses strategy, creative direction and execution so everything works toward one business objective.

05

Execute • Measure • Improve

We execute, measure performance, review learnings and use them to improve the next marketing cycle.

FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

Is the advertising budget included?

No. Ad spend is separate from the monthly agency fee in all three plans.

Can I choose a custom package?

Yes. We can discuss a customised scope based on your business goals and requirements.

Do you only manage Instagram?

No. Depending on the plan, we can manage multiple social platforms, Google Business Profile and selected e-commerce marketplaces.

Will you understand my business before creating content?

Yes. Business discovery and competitor research happen before the monthly marketing direction is built.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

Marketing results depend on the offer, market, competition, budget, product/service and customer behaviour. We focus on strategy, execution, measurement and improvement rather than arbitrary guarantees.

How do we communicate during the month?

Communication and strategy discussions are planned according to the selected package. Growth and Pro include one compulsory monthly strategy meeting.
